# Licensing

Vocab-U is licensed in three layers, because code, curated content and third-party
datasets cannot share one licence honestly.

## Application code — GPL-3.0-or-later

Everything under `apps/`, `packages/` and `tools/` unless a file says otherwise.
The full text is in `COPYING`. Copyleft is deliberate: a fork of a free community
app should stay free.

## Curated content and documentation — CC BY-SA 4.0

Everything under `docs/` and `tools/corpus/data/overrides/` — the human-written
glosses, example sentences and topic curation contributed to this project.

## Compiled corpus — inherits its sources

`vocab.db` is a build artifact derived from third-party open datasets. It carries the
obligations of whatever went into it. Every source, its licence and its attribution
requirement is enumerated in `tools/corpus/LICENSES.md`, which is regenerated by the
build and shipped inside the app under Settings → Open source licences.

A dataset whose terms are unclear does not go in. There is no "probably fine" tier.

## Imagery — CC0 or public domain only

Users publish share cards containing our imagery to social networks. Anything we ship
must be safe for them to do that with, which rules out most "free for personal use"
stock. Sources are recorded in `apps/mobile/assets/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
